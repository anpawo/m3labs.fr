5. How a project runs

5.1 The free call

A call of 30 minutes at most, by phone or by video, free and without commitment: you show the task and where its data lives, and M3 Labs tells you whether software is the right fix and what it would take.

5.2 The quote

Before any work starts, M3 Labs sends a written quote stating the scope, the deliverables, the price, the payment schedule and the delivery date. The project starts once the client accepts the quote in writing (a signature, or an email saying so) and, if the quote asks for one, pays the deposit. A quote is valid for 30 days unless it says otherwise.

5.3 Demos and delivery

The client sees the software run on its own data: once before it goes live for a Task, every week for an App or an Ecosystem. Delivery means handing over the source code, its documentation and every access and account the software needs.

5.4 Acceptance of the software

The client checks the delivered software against the quote and reports any gap in writing within the period the quote sets (15 days if it sets none). M3 Labs fixes each gap and delivers again. The software is accepted when that period ends with no gap reported, or when the client uses it for its work.

5.5 Changes

Work outside the quote's scope is quoted apart, in an amendment or a new quote, before it is done.

5.6 What the client provides

The client gives, in time, the access, data, documents and answers the project needs, and makes sure it holds the rights to them. A delay on the client's side moves the delivery date by as much.

The third-party services the software needs (hosting, domain names, APIs, AI models, licences) are paid by the client to their providers, in accounts opened in its name, unless the quote says otherwise.

7. Right of withdrawal

A consumer who accepts a quote at a distance (by email or online) or away from M3 Labs' premises has 14 days from that acceptance to withdraw, without giving a reason (articles L221-18 and following of the French Consumer Code). The same right belongs to a professional with five employees or fewer whose project lies outside its main activity (article L221-3).

To withdraw, write to contact@m3labs.fr before the 14 days end, saying so clearly.

Work starts within those 14 days only if the client asks for it expressly. A client who then withdraws pays for the work done until the withdrawal, in proportion to the price. Once the work is fully done at the client's express request, with the client's acknowledgement that it then loses this right, it can no longer withdraw (article L221-28).
