1. Introduction

This policy says what personal data M3 Labs handles when you visit m3labs.fr, sign in, book a call, write to M3 Labs or become a client, and how, under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the French Data Protection Act (loi Informatique et Libertés).

Controller: Marius ROUSSET EI (M3 Labs), SIREN 130 238 223, 8 avenue Robert Daugey, 13080 Aix-en-Provence, France. Contact: contact@m3labs.fr

M3 Labs is a one-person business: it has no data protection officer, and Marius Rousset answers every request himself.

2. Definitions

  • Personal data: any information about a person who can be identified, directly or not
  • Processing: anything done with that data (collecting, storing, reading, sending, deleting)
  • Controller: who decides why and how the data is processed
  • Processor: who processes data on a controller's behalf

The Site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising trackers. Its fonts are served from m3labs.fr itself.

3.3 Data in a project

The data a client gives access to during a project (its clients', its staff's, the people in its mails) is processed by M3 Labs as the client's processor, under section 12.1 of the terms. The client stays its controller.

3.4 Prospecting

M3 Labs may contact businesses it thinks it can help. It uses only what businesses publish themselves (company name, website, job offers, generic contact addresses) and the role of the person to contact, never a private person's data bought or scraped. Each message says where the data comes from and how to object.

5. What M3 Labs does with it

  • Writes back to you, and holds the call you booked
  • Sends quotes and invoices, and carries out projects
  • Keeps the records the law asks for

What M3 Labs does not do:

  • Sell, rent or swap your data
  • Build advertising profiles, or send newsletters you did not ask for
  • Train AI models on your data
  • Take decisions about you by automated means alone

8. Security

  • The Site is served over HTTPS only
  • Your data stays in the accounts of the services above, which only Marius Rousset uses; none of it is published
  • A project's data stays, wherever possible, in the client's own accounts, and only what the project needs is copied
  • A breach of personal data is reported to the CNIL within 72 hours, and to the people concerned when it puts them at high risk (articles 33 and 34 GDPR)

9.2 Complaint

If you think your rights are not respected, you may complain to the CNIL, 3 place de Fontenoy, TSA 80715, 75334 Paris Cedex 07, www.cnil.fr.

10. Data not collected from you

For prospecting (section 3.4), M3 Labs takes a business's name, website, job offers and generic contact address from what that business publishes, with the role of the person to contact. The first message to that business says so, with how to object (article 14 GDPR).

11. Cookies and local storage

m3labs.fr sets no cookies. It keeps one value in your browser's local storage, the language you picked, which never leaves your browser and needs no consent. The Google and Apple sign-in windows belong to those companies and follow their own rules.

12. Changes to this policy

This policy may change. Each new version is published on this page with its date and number, and clients are told of any change that matters by mail.
